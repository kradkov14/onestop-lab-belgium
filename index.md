The Belgium Living Lab is one of five OneSTOP Living Labs across Europe. It is a real-world environment for testing new detection methods, refining tools and informing invasive species management policy in Belgium.

A core stakeholder group identifies priority species and locations, tests new technologies, engages potential users and supports citizen science activities. Feedback from the Lab is used to improve the technologies and workflows, and the data it generates feeds into the OneSTOP data-driven prioritisation system.

Read more about the Lab's launch on the [OneSTOP website](https://onestop-project.eu/news/onestop-launches-belgian-living-lab-advance-invasive-species-monitoring).
